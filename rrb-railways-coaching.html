Assistant Loco Pilot

RRB ALP Coaching in Dibrugarh

The Assistant Loco Pilot (ALP) examination selects technical personnel for locomotive operations. The selection process involves a multi-stage Computer Based Test followed by Computer Based Aptitude Test (CBAT) and Document Verification.

CBT-1 Preparation

  • Mathematics: Number system, BODMAS, ratio, percentage, algebra, geometry.
  • General Intelligence and Reasoning: Analogies, coding-decoding, relationships, direction sense.
  • General Science: Physics, Chemistry and Life Sciences of 10th standard level.
  • General Awareness: Current affairs, science and technology, sports, culture, personalities.

CBT-2 Preparation

  • Part A: Mathematics, General Intelligence & Reasoning, Basic Science and Engineering.
  • Basic Science & Engineering: Engineering drawing, units and measurements, mass, weight and density, work power and energy, speed and velocity, heat and temperature.
  • Part B (Qualifying): Relevant technical trade syllabus as prescribed by DGET for the candidate's engineering discipline or trade qualification.
Eligibility Notice: ALP eligibility strictly requires prescribed technical qualifications (Matriculation/SSLC plus ITI/Act Apprenticeship or Diploma/Degree in Engineering) as specified in official RRB recruitment notifications. Candidates without prescribed technical qualifications are not eligible for ALP solely on the basis of Class 10.

Digital Testing Skills

Prepare for RRB Computer-Based Tests

Modern RRB examinations are conducted entirely online. Adapting to the computer-based testing format is critical to avoid exam-day stress and maximize scores.

CBT Interface Familiarity

Learn to navigate between question palettes, switch language views, mark questions for review, and clear response choices smoothly.

Time Management & Pacing

RRB tests allocate 90 minutes for 100 questions. Practising under timed conditions trains you to allocate seconds wisely per question.

Calculation Speed & Accuracy

With negative marking of 1/3rd for incorrect answers, accuracy is paramount. Regular objective practice reduces calculation errors.

Disclaimer: Computer-based test practice sessions at Scholar Academy are educational training exercises developed for exam preparation and are not official Railway Recruitment Board tests.
