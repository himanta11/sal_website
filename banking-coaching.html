Target Recruitments

Banking Exams We Prepare You For

Specialized coaching batches tailored to the syllabus structure and competition level of premier national banking exams.

Probationary Officer

1. IBPS PO

Recruitment for Management Trainee / Probationary Officer positions across 11 nationalized public sector banks (PNB, Canara Bank, Bank of Baroda, etc.).

Preparation Requirements: Three-tier selection: 1-hour fast-track Prelims, comprehensive Mains (Data Analysis, High-Level Reasoning, Descriptive English essay & letter), followed by 1-on-1 interview training.
Clerical Cadre

2. IBPS Clerk

Large-scale annual recruitment for Customer Service Associates and clerical cadres across participating nationalized banks nationwide.

Preparation Requirements: Demands blistering speed and 95%+ accuracy in 100-question Prelims (60 mins), followed by Mains testing Quantitative Aptitude, Reasoning, English, and General Financial Awareness.
State Bank of India

3. SBI PO

The most prestigious and demanding banking career examination in India, conducted by the country's largest public sector lender.

Preparation Requirements: Requires mastery over unfamiliar puzzle variations, critical reasoning, complex Caselet DI, digital banking trends, Group Exercises (GE), and a rigorous Personal Interview round.
Junior Associate

4. SBI Clerk

Recruitment for Junior Associates (Customer Support & Sales) in State Bank of India branches across Assam and the Northeast region.

Preparation Requirements: Intensive focus on calculation shortcuts (simplification, arithmetic series), error spotting, speed reading, financial awareness, and Regional Language Proficiency Test (LPT) support.
