Examination Blueprint

Understanding the APSC Combined Competitive Exam (CCE)

The Assam Public Service Commission (APSC) conducts the Combined Competitive Examination (CCE) annually to recruit administrative leaders for prestigious civil services across Assam. Candidates who qualify hold foundational governance positions shaping Assam's development trajectory.

Following the adoption of the UPSC exam pattern, APSC CCE emphasizes analytical understanding, multidimensional thinking, and expressive writing rather than rote memorization.

Stage 1: Prelims Objective Screening (400 Marks)
Stage 2: Mains Written Descriptive (1500 Marks)
Stage 3: Interview Personality Test (180 Marks)

Elite Services Recruited via APSC

Qualifying the APSC CCE opens career opportunities across coveted Grade-A & Grade-B state gazetted cadres:

  • Assam Civil Service (ACS Jr. Grade)
  • Assam Police Service (APS Jr. Grade)
  • Block Development Officer (BDO)
  • Assam Land & Revenue Service (ALRS)
  • Superintendent of Taxes
  • Assistant Registrar of Co-op Societies
  • Labour Officer / Inspector of Taxes
  • District Transport Officer (DTO)
Eligibility Note: Candidates must hold a graduate degree from a recognized university and fulfill domicile and age criteria specified in official APSC notifications.
Stage 1 Preparation

APSC Prelims Foundation Program

The Preliminary exam comprises two objective papers: GS Paper I (General Studies - 200 Marks) and GS Paper II (CSAT - 200 Marks, qualifying with 33%). Our curriculum covers all 10 core subjects with foundational depth and test drills.

Indian Polity & Governance

Constitutional framework, Fundamental Rights, Panchayati Raj, judiciary, statutory bodies, and administrative reforms.

Assam History, Culture & Heritage

Varman to Ahom dynasties, British annexation, Assam's freedom struggle leaders, festivals, tribes, and cultural traditions.

Indian History & National Movement

Ancient, Medieval, Modern Indian history, socio-religious reform movements, and the Indian Freedom Struggle.

Geography of India & Assam

Physical features, Brahmaputra & Barak river systems, monsoon meteorology, minerals, forests, and demographic patterns.

Indian Economy & Assam Budget

Macroeconomics, inflation, monetary policy, agricultural development, state budget analysis, and key Assam economic initiatives.

Environment, Ecology & Biodiversity

Assam's National Parks, wildlife conservation (Rhino, Tiger, Elephant reserves), climate change, and environmental laws.

General Science & Technology

Everyday science concepts, defense tech, space missions, biotechnology, artificial intelligence, and digital public infrastructure.

National & International Current Affairs

Daily government policies, bilateral summits, bilateral treaties, national rankings, indices, and major appointments.

Mental Ability, Logic & Reasoning

Analytical reasoning, syllogisms, sequence drills, data interpretation, and pattern comprehension for CSAT Paper II.

Basic Numeracy & English Comprehension

Class 10 arithmetic, percentages, ratios, data sufficiency, and reading comprehension passages for qualifying CSAT confidence.

Stage 2 Mastery

APSC Mains Comprehensive Mentorship

The Mains examination is the deciding factor for your rank and cadre allocation. Totaling 1,500 marks across 6 descriptive papers, it evaluates conceptual clarity, logical coherence, balanced argumentation, and effective articulation.

Paper 1: Essay Writing 250 Marks
Paper 2: General Studies I (History, Art & Geo) 250 Marks
Paper 3: General Studies II (Polity, Governance, IR) 250 Marks
Paper 4: General Studies III (Economy, Sci & Tech, Env) 250 Marks
Paper 5: General Studies IV (Ethics, Integrity & Aptitude) 250 Marks
Paper 6: General Studies V (Assam-Specific Paper) 250 Marks
