Exam Structure & Stages

Complete Preparation for APSC CCE

The Assam Public Service Commission Combined Competitive Examination (APSC CCE) is conducted across three stages. Understanding each stage is vital for planning an effective preparation schedule.

Stage 1: Screening Test

A. APSC Preliminary Examination

The preliminary examination assesses broad General Studies knowledge and is used as a screening stage according to the applicable APSC notification. It consists of objective multiple-choice questions (MCQs).

Suggested Preparation Areas:

Indian History & National Movement Indian & World Geography Assam History & Geography Indian Polity & Constitution Indian Economy General Science Environment & Ecology Current Affairs Assam-specific General Knowledge General Mental Ability
Stage 2: Written Examination

B. APSC Main Examination

The Main examination requires analytical understanding, structured answers, descriptive writing skills, and deep subject knowledge across various General Studies papers and Essay.

Suggested Preparation Areas:

Essay Writing General Studies (GS I to IV) Indian Society & Governance Indian Constitution & Polity Economy & Development Science & Technology Environment Ethics & Integrity Assam-specific Issues (GS-V) National & International Developments
Stage 3: Personality Test

C. Interview / Personality Test

The final stage evaluates the candidate's personal suitability for a career in public administration by an interview board. It assesses clarity of thought, integrity, and social awareness.

Key Areas of Focus:

Communication Skills Awareness of Assam & India Current Affairs & Policy Issues Analytical Thinking Confidence & Mental Alertness Understanding of Public Administration

Important Note: Candidates should always verify the current APSC CCE examination pattern, paper structure, marks distribution, and qualifying criteria from the latest official notification published by the Assam Public Service Commission.
