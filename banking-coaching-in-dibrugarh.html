Target Recruitments

Banking Exams We Cover at Dibrugarh Centre

Our integrated banking foundation curriculum prepares candidates simultaneously across all major public sector bank entrance exams conducted annually:

Officer Cadre

IBPS PO (Probationary Officer)

Recruitment examination for Probationary Officers and Management Trainees across participating public sector banks. Stage-by-stage guidance for Prelims, Mains, and Interview.

  • Prelims: Reasoning (35 Q), Quantitative Aptitude (35 Q), English (30 Q)
  • Mains: Reasoning & Computer, Data Analysis, General & Banking Awareness, English
  • Descriptive Test: Essay & Letter Writing Evaluation
Clerical Cadre

IBPS Clerk (Customer Support Associate)

State-wise recruitment for clerical positions in participating banks across Assam. Emphasis on accuracy and ultra-high-speed preliminary clearance.

  • Two-tier examination (Prelims + Mains) with zero interview barrier
  • Sectional time allocation mastery (20 minutes per Prelims section)
  • Assam regional cut-off analysis and target score planning
Premier Bank

SBI PO (State Bank of India)

The most prestigious banking recruitment in India with analytical, high-difficulty level reasoning, higher-order quantitative aptitude, and rigorous psychometric evaluations.

  • Deep focus on advanced DI (Radar, Funnel, Missing graphs)
  • Descriptive English test evaluation and grammar sharpening
  • Group Exercises (GE) & Personal Interview preparation
High Vacancy

SBI Clerk (Junior Associate)

Direct entry point into State Bank of India's branch network across Upper Assam. Heavy weightage on high-speed simplification, numerical ability, and current affairs.

  • 100-mark preliminary test practice with time management
  • Mains General & Financial Awareness question drills
  • Local language proficiency verification assistance

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions About Banking Coaching in Dibrugarh

Common queries from banking aspirants across Dibrugarh, Tinsukia, and Upper Assam:

Which banking examinations are covered at the Dibrugarh centre?

Our Dibrugarh banking coaching programme covers IBPS PO, IBPS Clerk, SBI PO, SBI Clerk, IBPS RRB (Officer Scale-I and Office Assistant for Assam Gramin Vikash Bank), and RBI Assistant examinations through a unified, high-level curriculum.

What is the eligibility for appearing in bank exams?

Any graduate from a recognized university in any discipline (Arts, Science, Commerce, Engineering, etc.) is eligible for IBPS PO, IBPS Clerk, and SBI exams. Final year degree students can also enrol in our batches to prepare before completing graduation.

How are Quantitative Aptitude shortcuts and Vedic maths taught?

We dedicate initial weeks specifically to mental calculation techniques, fraction-to-percentage tables, root-finding methods, and fast approximation tricks. This enables students to solve 30+ questions within the 20-minute sectional cut-off.

Are mock tests conducted on computer terminals?

Yes. In addition to weekly classroom assessments, students practise in our on-campus computer lab with timed CBT simulations replicating the exact interface of IBPS and SBI online examination software.

What if I miss a classroom lecture?

Students who miss a class due to illness or exams can attend backup doubt-clearing sessions with our subject faculty and access class study handouts from our student desk.

Where is the Dibrugarh centre located and how can I visit?

Scholar Academy of Learning is located at Lachit Nagar, Dibrugarh, Assam 786001. You can call +91 86387 57905 to book an appointment or visit between 8:00 AM and 7:30 PM Monday through Saturday.
