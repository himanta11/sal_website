Why Is Banking Exam Preparation Different?

Banking exams conducted by the Institute of Banking Personnel Selection (IBPS) and the State Bank of India (SBI) test your mental agility under strict sectional time limits. Success depends entirely on two core metrics: Speed and Accuracy.

Aspirants face four major sections:

  • Quantitative Aptitude: Fast calculation, Data Interpretation (DI), and mental arithmetic.
  • Reasoning Ability: Complex seating arrangements, circular puzzles, syllogisms, and coding-decoding.
  • English Language: Reading comprehension, grammar rules, and vocabulary in financial contexts.
  • General & Banking Awareness: Current affairs, RBI monetary policy updates, and banking terminology.

Mastering these sections within 60 minutes in the Preliminary phase requires structured coaching, shortcut calculation hacks, and continuous computer-based testing.
