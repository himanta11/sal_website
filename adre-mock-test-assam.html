ADRE Grade III & Grade IV Preparation Strategy

The State Level Recruitment Commission (SLRC) conducts the Assam Direct Recruitment Examination (ADRE) across Grade III and Grade IV categories. Cracking this highly competitive recruitment requires focused practice on the exact syllabus, question distribution, and marking patterns.

CATEGORY 1

ADRE Grade III Mock Tests

Designed for Graduate & Higher Secondary qualification posts (Junior Assistant, Field Assistant, LDA, Stenographer, etc.):

  • Social Studies: Indian History, Assam History, Indian Polity, Geography of Assam & India, and General Science.
  • General Knowledge & Current Affairs: Assam Government schemes, National & International events, sports, literature and culture.
  • Logical Reasoning & Mental Ability: Series, coding-decoding, analogies, direction sense, and analytical reasoning.
  • General Mathematics: Arithmetic, percentages, ratio & proportion, profit & loss, algebra, and geometry.
  • General English: Vocabulary, sentence correction, synonyms/antonyms, idioms, and reading comprehension.
  • Assessment Mode: 150 questions (or post-specific marks), 2 hours duration, negative marking applicable.
CATEGORY 2

ADRE Grade IV Mock Tests

Tailored for Class 8, Class 10 (HSLC), and Class 10 with ITI qualification posts (Peon, Chowkidar, Cleaner, Workshop Helper, etc.):

  • English: Basic grammar, word usage, fill in the blanks, spelling checks, and vocabulary.
  • Mathematics: Basic arithmetic calculations, fractions, decimals, simple interest, unitary method.
  • General Knowledge: Assam geography, prominent personalities, historical events, cultural heritage, and basic science.
  • Mental Ability: Simple puzzles, pattern recognition, number series, and relationship tests.
  • Bilingual Accessibility: Available in Assamese and English mediums.
  • Assessment Mode: 135 questions, 135 marks, 2 hours duration, with standard negative marking.

Why Regular Mock Test Practice is Vital for ADRE Success

Many aspirants study the syllabus thoroughly but score below the cutoff on exam day due to poor time allocation, exam anxiety, or careless bubbling errors on the OMR sheet. Consistent mock test practice directly solves these challenges:

Master Speed & Accuracy

Solve 135–150 questions in 120 minutes without rushing through mathematics or misreading tricky negative questions.

Eliminate Negative Marking

Learn when to attempt calculated risks and when to leave uncertain questions to protect your hard-earned score.

Audit Weak Sections

Instantly identify whether you are losing marks in Assam History, Mensuration, or English Vocabulary, allowing targeted revision.

OMR Sheet Experience

Physical bubbling takes 10 to 15 minutes of your exam time. Offline practice ensures you never run out of time at the final bell.
