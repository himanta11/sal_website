The Common Pitfalls in Assam Exam Preparation

Most aspirants spend months gathering photocopied notes and reading PDFs, yet struggle on exam day due to three major hurdles:

  • Unverified Answers in Circulated PDFs: Study groups often share past question papers with incorrect answer keys, leading students to memorize flawed concepts.
  • Untimed Self-Study: Solving practice questions without a countdown timer fails to simulate actual examination pressure.
  • No Realistic Benchmarking: Offline self-testing does not indicate where your preparation stands relative to other candidates across the state.

The PYQscore App was developed specifically to solve these fundamental challenges through a streamlined mobile testing interface.
