Frequently Asked Questions

ADRE Coaching FAQs: Dibrugarh & Tezpur

Common questions answered regarding eligibility, exam syllabus, and batch schedules.

What is the educational qualification required for ADRE Grade III and IV?
For Grade III posts, candidates must possess either a Bachelor's Degree or a 10+2 (Higher Secondary) certificate, depending on the specific post (such as Junior Assistant or Field Assistant). For Grade IV posts, candidates must have passed either Class VIII or HSLC (Class 10).
What kind of test series and practice are provided?
We conduct weekly full-length classroom practice tests and computer-based mock examinations strictly aligned with the latest SLRC exam pattern, helping students build speed, accuracy, and negative marking control.
Can students attend weekend batches?
Yes, both our Dibrugarh and Tezpur centres offer dedicated Saturday & Sunday weekend batches for students pursuing degrees or working candidates.
Is Assam GK given special priority?
Yes, Assam GK accounts for the highest single weightage in ADRE. Our syllabus provides exhaustive coverage of Assam History, Geography, National Parks, Tea Industry, Oil Sector, Festivals, and Government Schemes.
