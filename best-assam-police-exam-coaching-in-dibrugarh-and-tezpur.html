The Challenge of the Assam Police Exam (SLPRB)

The Assam Police recruitment process tests candidates across multiple rigorous stages:

  • Written Examination: Testing Logical Reasoning & Aptitude, General English, General Studies, Elementary Arithmetic, and Assam General Knowledge (History, Geography, Polity, Culture).
  • Physical Standard Test (PST) & Physical Efficiency Test (PET): Endurance running, long jump, and race timings that determine eligibility.
  • Document Verification & Viva-Voce: Interview round assessing confidence, communication, and personality.

A balanced approach that sharpens your written test scores while maintaining physical readiness is essential for securing a top rank in the merit list.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What is the minimum qualification for the Assam Police exam?

For Constable (Unarmed Branch/Armed Branch), candidates generally require HSLC (10th) or HSSLC (12th) pass depending on the cadre. For Sub-Inspector (SI), a Bachelor's degree from a recognized university is mandatory.

Does Scholar Academy provide mock test series for Assam Police?
