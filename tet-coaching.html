Core TET Discipline

Master Child Development and Pedagogy for TET

Child Development and Pedagogy (CDP) is the cornerstone of Teacher Eligibility Tests. Conceptual clarity in psychological principles and their classroom applications determines success.

Child Development Principles

  • Meaning & Principles: Continuous growth, cephalocaudal & proximodistal trends.
  • Growth vs Development: Quantitative and qualitative indicators of development.
  • Stages of Childhood: Infancy, early childhood, late childhood, and adolescent transitions.
  • Physical & Motor Development: Fine and gross motor skills in primary learners.
  • Cognitive & Language Development: Thought processes, memory, and speech acquisition.
  • Social & Emotional Development: Peer relations, emotional regulation, and self-concept.
  • Individual Differences: Understanding learner diversity across intelligence, personality, and aptitude.

Learning Theories & Pedagogy

  • Piaget's Cognitive Theory: Sensorimotor, preoperational, concrete, and formal operational stages.
  • Vygotsky's Socio-Cultural Theory: Zone of Proximal Development (ZPD) & scaffolding.
  • Kohlberg's Moral Development: Pre-conventional, conventional, and post-conventional stages.
  • Constructivist Classrooms: Active knowledge construction, inquiry-based learning.
  • Inclusive Education: Catering to children with special needs (CWSN) and learning disabilities.
  • Child-Centred Teaching: Shifting from rote memorization to experiential discovery.
  • Assessment & Evaluation: Formative vs summative evaluation, CCE, and diagnostic remediation.
At Scholar Academy, our CDP classroom sessions emphasize practical case studies, pedagogical dilemma questions, and previous-year TET patterns to develop robust conceptual clarity.
Language Teaching Methodologies

Language Pedagogy for Assam TET Preparation

Language questions assess both grammatical correctness and the methodology of teaching languages to young learners.

Assamese Language Pedagogy

Preparation for Language I encompasses structural language grasp and mother-tongue teaching methods:

  • Reading comprehension of unseen prose and poetry
  • Assamese grammar (Sandhi, Samas, Karak, Bibhakti, Pratyay)
  • Vocabulary development, synonyms, and antonyms
  • Language acquisition vs language learning principles
  • Four fundamental language skills: Listening, Speaking, Reading & Writing (LSRW)
  • Challenges of teaching language in diverse classroom environments
  • Remedial teaching strategies and continuous language evaluation

English Language Pedagogy

Preparation for Language II focuses on English language acquisition as a second language:

  • Reading comprehension passages with inference-based questions
  • Grammar fundamentals, parts of speech, active/passive voice, tenses
  • Vocabulary enhancement, idioms, and contextual word usage
  • Principles of second-language teaching & communicative approaches
  • Role of listening and speaking in building English fluency
  • Evaluating language comprehension and proficiency
  • Addressing common pronunciation and grammatical errors
