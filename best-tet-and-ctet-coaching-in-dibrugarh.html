Understanding the TET & CTET Exam Pattern

Both examinations evaluate pedagogical maturity and fundamental curriculum understanding across 150 objective Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs) without negative marking.

Structure of Paper 1 (Primary Stage: Classes I–V)

  • Child Development and Pedagogy (CDP): 30 Questions (30 Marks)
  • Language I (Assamese / Bengali / Bodo / Alternative English): 30 Questions (30 Marks)
  • Language II (English): 30 Questions (30 Marks)
  • Mathematics: 30 Questions (30 Marks)
  • Environmental Studies (EVS): 30 Questions (30 Marks)

Structure of Paper 2 (Elementary Stage: Classes VI–VIII)

  • Child Development and Pedagogy (CDP): 30 Questions (30 Marks)
  • Language I & Language II: 60 Questions (60 Marks total)
  • Subject Specialization: 60 Questions (60 Marks)
    • Option A: Mathematics & Science (for Math/Science teachers)
    • Option B: Social Studies / Social Science (for Arts/Humanities teachers)

Why Many Candidates Struggle with TET & CTET Exams

  • Pedagogy Is Not Theory Alone: Questions in Child Development & Pedagogy are case-study driven (e.g., Piaget, Vygotsky, Kohlberg theories applied to real classroom situations). Direct rote memorization fails here.
  • Time Management in Reading Comprehension: Long unseen passages in Language I and II consume excessive time if candidates lack speed-reading strategies.
  • Application Math & Environmental Science: School teachers frequently lose marks in Class 6–8 level applied mathematics and regional environmental context questions.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1. Is CTET valid for teaching jobs in Assam government schools?

Yes, CTET qualifications are generally recognized alongside state TET for state teacher recruitments in Assam, as per government policy directives, in addition to central schools like KVS, NVS, and Army Public Schools.

Q2. Can B.Ed / D.El.Ed appearing candidates apply for TET & CTET coaching?

Yes. Candidates enrolled in teacher training courses (final year or pursuing semesters) are eligible to appear in the examinations and benefit substantially by starting their preparation early.

Q3. How long does the TET / CTET syllabus preparation take?

A dedicated preparation period of 3 to 4 months with regular mock testing is usually optimal to thoroughly cover Child Development & Pedagogy, language sections, and domain subjects.
