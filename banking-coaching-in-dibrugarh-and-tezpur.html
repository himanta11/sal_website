Banking Course Syllabus: What Will You Study?

A well-planned banking exam preparation programme must cover the complete breadth of subjects required across Prelims and Mains. At Scholar Academy of Learning, the curriculum is structured into core modules:

Quantitative Aptitude & Numerical Ability

Fast mental arithmetic, approximation techniques, and advanced data visualization:

  • Number systems, simplification, and fast approximation
  • Percentage, ratio and proportion, average, and partnership
  • Profit, loss, and discount; simple and compound interest
  • Time and work, pipes and cisterns; time, speed, and distance (trains, boats & streams)
  • Data Interpretation (DI): Bar graphs, Line graphs, Pie charts, Radar charts, and Caselet DI
  • Missing and wrong number series, quadratic equations

Reasoning Ability & Logical Thinking

High-scoring puzzles and analytical problem-solving modules:

  • Coding-decoding, blood relations, and direction-distance tests
  • Syllogisms (standard, reverse, and 'only a few' patterns) and inequalities
  • Seating arrangements: Circular, square, linear, and parallel rows
  • Complex puzzles: Floor-based, box-based, scheduling, and multi-variable puzzles
  • Machine input-output, order and ranking, and critical/logical reasoning

English Language & Descriptive Writing

Grammar mastery, comprehension speed, and descriptive evaluation:

  • Grammar fundamentals, parts of speech, and error detection
  • Reading comprehension passages with inference-based questions
  • Cloze tests, sentence rearrangement, and para jumbles
  • Fill in the blanks, phrase replacement, and vocabulary building
  • Descriptive writing for PO: Formal letter drafting and essay writing review

Banking Awareness and Current Affairs

Core banking knowledge and dynamic economic affairs:

  • Structure of the Indian banking and financial system
  • Reserve Bank of India (RBI): Functions, monetary policy tools (Repo, Reverse Repo, CRR, SLR)
  • Banking terminology, Basel norms, NPA management, and Priority Sector Lending (PSL)
  • Union Budget, economic surveys, and national/international financial news
  • Government financial inclusion schemes (PMJDY, Mudra, APY, KCC)

Computer Knowledge (for RRB & Specific Mains)

Fundamentals of computers, hardware, operating systems, networking, internet security, and office applications.

How to Prepare for Banking Exams Effectively

Joining coaching is the foundation; pairing it with a disciplined daily routine ensures success. Here is our recommended 7-step roadmap:

1

Understand the Examination Pattern

Review the exact stages (Prelims, Mains, Interview), marking schemes, negative marking penalties (0.25 marks), and sectional timing cutoffs.

2

Strengthen Your Fundamentals

Master Vedic math techniques, squares/cubes up to 30, fractional percentages, and grammar rules before attempting full-length timed tests.

3

Practise Every Day Under Timer Pressure

Solve 30-50 topic-wise questions daily with a timer. In banking examinations, speed with 90%+ accuracy decides your merit rank.

4

Follow Financial & National Current Affairs

Read daily financial news, understand RBI circulars, and review monthly general awareness summaries for Mains scoring.

5

Take Regular Computer-Based Mock Tests

Utilize the academy's online mock test portal to simulate authentic exam-hall pressure and test navigation.

6

Thoroughly Analyse Your Test Performance

Spend twice as much time reviewing solutions as taking the test. Identify question-selection errors, unattempted traps, and repeated calculation slips.

7

Revise Frequently with Short Notes

Keep a dedicated formula and shortcut diary for quick weekend revision before the real examination day.
