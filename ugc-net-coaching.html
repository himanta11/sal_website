Get answers to questions regarding eligibility, exam syllabus, and batch schedules.

What is the eligibility criteria for UGC NET and SLET?
Candidates must have secured at least 55% marks (50% for SC/ST/OBC-NCL/PwD/Third Gender) in their Master's Degree or equivalent examination from recognized universities. Final year master's students can also appear provisionally.
How important is Paper 1 in qualifying for JRF?
Paper 1 is the primary differentiator for JRF qualification. Candidates who score 75+ in Paper 1 consistently achieve the top 1% percentile required for Junior Research Fellowship.
Does this course prepare for SLET (North East Region)?
Yes! SLET North East Paper 1 syllabus is identical to UGC NET Paper 1. Our syllabus, test series, and notes prepare you thoroughly for both examinations simultaneously.
