High-Yield Syllabus Breakdown: What the 2022 & 2024 Papers Reveal

A comparative review of the 2022 and 2024 question sets highlights five consistent subjects evaluated by the examination commission:

1. Social Studies, Assam GK & Heritage

  • Ahom Administration & Resistance: Sukaphaa’s establishment, Paik system, Lachit Borphukan and the Battle of Saraighat, and the Treaty of Yandabo (1826).
  • Peasant & Freedom Movements: Phulaguri Dhewa (1861), Patharughat uprising (1894), Kushal Konwar, Kanaklata Barua, and Maniram Dewan.
  • Geography & Biodiversity: Brahmaputra and Barak river tributaries, National Parks (Kaziranga, Manas, Raimona, Dehing Patkai), and oil/coal reserves.
  • Culture & Art: Srimanta Sankardeva, Satriya dance, Bihu festivals, and Assamese literary milestones (Jonaki and Orunodoi eras).

2. General English

  • Applied Grammar: Subject-verb concord, tense consistency, error-spotting, and sentence restructuring.
  • Prepositions & Idioms: Focus on fixed prepositions (e.g., refrain from, eligible for, adhere to) and standard phrases.
  • Vocabulary: Direct synonyms, antonyms, and one-word substitutions.
  • Reading Comprehension: Short, factual passages requiring data extraction and contextual understanding.

3. Mathematics & Numerical Ability

  • Class 10 Arithmetic: Percentages, Profit & Loss, Simple and Compound Interest, Ratio & Proportion, and Averages.
  • Speed, Time & Work: Train-crossing problems, boat and stream upstream/downstream formulas, and unit work efficiency.
  • Mensuration & DI: 2D/3D surface area calculations and basic chart/table interpretation.

4. Mental Ability & Logical Reasoning

  • Verbal Logic: Blood relations, coding-decoding, number/alphabet series, and direction tests.
  • Non-Verbal Reasoning: Mirror images, figure analogies, and embedded patterns.

5. Current Affairs

  • State Welfare Initiatives: Flagship schemes of the Government of Assam across education, health, agriculture, and youth empowerment.
  • National & Regional Honors: Assam Baibhav, Assam Saurav, Padma awardees from the Northeast, and national sports achievements.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Are the ADRE Grade 3 question papers and answer keys on www.pyqscore.in completely free?

Yes. All question paper PDFs and official answer keys for both 2022 and 2024 batches can be downloaded 100% free of charge from www.pyqscore.in.

Is there negative marking in the ADRE Grade 3 examination?

Yes. Negative marking applies for every incorrect response (typically 0.25 to 0.50 marks per wrong answer). Practicing on the PYQscore app helps you master negative marking management by discouraging random guesses.

Are questions repeated from the 2022 and 2024 ADRE papers?

While exact static GK questions are rarely repeated word-for-word, question structures, historical event chronologies, grammar patterns, and arithmetic problem models repeat heavily across cycles.

In which mediums are the ADRE Grade 3 question papers printed?

Official question papers are provided in four languages: Assamese, Bengali, Bodo, and English.
