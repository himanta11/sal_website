Assam Police Written Examination Pattern

The written test conducted by the State Level Police Recruitment Board (SLPRB) tests candidates on broad general competence, reasoning ability, quantitative aptitude, and state-specific knowledge.

Sub-Inspector (SI) Written Exam

  • Total Marks: 100 Marks (100 Multiple Choice Questions).
  • Duration: 3 Hours (180 Minutes).
  • Marking Scheme: 1 mark per correct answer, -0.50 mark penalty for each wrong answer.
  • Subjects:
    • Logical Reasoning, Aptitude & Comprehension (40 Questions)
    • Matters relating to History and Culture of Assam & India (30 Questions)
    • General Knowledge (30 Questions)

Constable (UB & AB) Written Exam

  • Total Marks: 45–50 Marks (depending on notification category).
  • Duration: 2 Hours (120 Minutes).
  • Medium: Assamese, Bodo, Bengali, and English.
  • Subjects:
    • Elementary Arithmetic & Mathematics
    • General English & Grammar
    • Logical Reasoning & Mental Ability
    • Assam History, Geography, Polity & Economy
    • General Awareness & Current Affairs
